About Neuozo

Neuozo is a leading provider of ISO 27001 auditing services, dedicated to helping organizations protect their information assets and achieve compliance with international standards.

Our team of certified auditors brings extensive experience across various industries, ensuring that your information security management system (ISMS) meets the highest standards of security and compliance.

We understand that every organization is unique, which is why we tailor our approach to your specific needs, ensuring a smooth and effective auditing process.

Our Services

ISO 27001 Gap Analysis

Identify gaps in your current information security practices compared to ISO 27001 requirements and receive actionable recommendations.

Certification Audits

Comprehensive Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits to assess your ISMS against ISO 27001 standards for certification purposes.

Surveillance Audits
